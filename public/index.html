Multispectral Formula Lab: NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)

Sentinel-2 monitors reflectance across 13 spectral bands. Healthy vegetation strongly absorbs visible Red light (Band 4, 665nm) for chlorophyll photosynthesis and strongly reflects Near-Infrared (Band 8, 842nm) through spongey leaf mesophyll scattering.

SECTION 1

1. ESA Copernicus Satellite Constellation

The European Space Agency's Sentinel-2A and 2B twin satellites provide multispectral optical imagery with a 5-day revisit cycle and 10-meter ground resolution. This allows real-time, non-destructive monitoring of crop vigour, moisture dynamics, and localized stress across Maharashtra's 36 districts.

SECTION 2

2. NDVI Crop Vigour & Biophysics

Chlorophyll actively absorbs Red light (Band 4) for photosynthesis while spongey leaf mesophyll reflects Near-Infrared (Band 8). The normalized formula \((NIR - Red)/(NIR + Red)\) produces values from -1.0 to +1.0. Values between 0.60–0.90 signify dense, vigorous crop canopy.

SECTION 3

3. Surface Humidity & Soil Moisture

Canopy boundary humidity controls plant evapotranspiration and fungal disease risk (powdery mildew / downy mildew). Synchronizing topsoil moisture (0–5 cm) with short-wave infrared (SWIR Band 11) prevents water stress and guides precision drip scheduling.

SECTION 4

4. Agricultural GHG Emissions (IPCC Tier-1/2)

Quantifies field greenhouse gas fluxes: Nitrous Oxide (\(N_2O\), GWP 273x) from synthetic nitrogen fertilization, Methane (\(CH_4\), GWP 28x) from flooded anaerobic rice paddies along river basins, and Carbon Dioxide (\(CO_2\)) from soil respiration.

SECTION 5

5. Multi-Date Phenology Growth Curves

Tracking the Day of Year (DOY) phenology reveals crop trajectory: starting low at Pre-Sowing (DOY 102), rising during Jointing (DOY 169), peaking at Tasseling/Reproduction (DOY 205), and steadily decreasing toward Maturity (DOY 251) and Harvest (DOY 287).

SECTION 6

6. Climate-Smart Precision Interventions

Satellite telemetry empowers precision dosing: Variable-Rate Nitrogen Fertigation (VRF) to curb \(N_2O\), Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) in rice paddies to slash \(CH_4\) by up to 48%, and root-zone drip targeting for water conservation.
